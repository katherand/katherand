Latin American Art & Cultural Heritage Preservation

An end-to-end digital preservation workflow created to capture, color-correct, and transcribe physical exhibition catalogs documenting regional Latin American art collections. ARC-DIG-02
Public Digital Archives & Donor Ingestion Framework

An automated workstation framework tailored for public library digital archives and local history collections. Handles incoming community media—such as USB drives, hard drives, or disk images from local donors—by staging media safely, verifying file formats, and executing privacy audits to protect sensitive personal data prior to public accessioning.
